Harvest & Co. is a bridge connecting conscious city kitchens with certified regenerative growers, heirloom seed preservers, and traditional oil presses.

Our Mission

Pure, Honest Food as Nature Intended

Modern commercial produce is often harvested prematurely, treated with artificial ripening agents, and stored in cold lockers for weeks. We believe food should be harvested when fully ripened on the plant under natural sunlight.

Every single fruit, leafy green, spice, and drop of oil in our pantry is sourced directly from ethical regional farms within 100 km, ensuring 0 chemical pesticide residues and peak biological vitality.

"He who plants a seed trusts in tomorrow."
— Harvest & Co. Farmer Creed

100% Bio-Organic

Composted neem, cow dung, and natural micro-flora enrich our living soils without synthetic NPK fertilizers.

Fair Farmer Trade

We cut out greedy middlemen, ensuring our partner growers receive up to 80% of retail price directly.
